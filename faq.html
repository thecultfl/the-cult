Frequently Asked Questions

Curious about The CULT? Here's a little more about who we are, what we believe, and what we're up to.

The mission of The CULT is connection. Connection to each other, to our planet, and to ourselves.

We are living through a loneliness epidemic. Roughly half of American adults experience measurable loneliness. We spend our time in online spaces because it is easier.

Our vision is a community built intentionally to foster personal connections with the people around us, a deeper connection with our planet, and a stronger connection to ourselves.

The CULT is a community. Some members prefer social group, club, or even intentional community.

Our name, The CULT, is an acronym that sums up our mission well:

Connection • Unity • Love • Teaching

The dictionary defines a cult as “a group or sect bound together by veneration of the same thing, person, ideal, etc.” In that sense, some members may consider us a cult because we are bound by our shared mission of connection.

We're a cult in the same way that Taylor Swift fans, Jeep owners, and smokers are cults of their own.

We honor our mission in many different ways, from casual gatherings to Celestial Celebrations.

We host seed exchanges, clothing exchanges, women's circles, educational workshops, creative gatherings, and so much more.
